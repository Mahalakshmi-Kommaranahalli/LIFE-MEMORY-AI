Core Capabilities

Engineered for Persistent Intelligence

Traditional LLMs reset their memory on every session. Life Memory AI gives your AI long-term semantic persistence.

Semantic Vector Memory

Powered by high-dimensional embeddings and pgvector cosine similarity search to retrieve relevant memories even when queries use different wording.

Memory Relationship Graph

NetworkX-powered graph engine connects related goals, skills, projects, and life events, visualizing your personal knowledge graph interactively.

Conflict & Evolution Tracking

When preferences or career paths evolve, the system flags contradictions and preserves chronological validity instead of destructively overwriting facts.

Document Intelligence

Upload PDF resumes, thesis proposals, certificates, and notes. PyMuPDF extracts text, indexes content, and auto-generates memories with user approval.

Chronological Timeline

An interactive timeline tracks your career milestones, degrees, certifications, and personal accomplishments chronologically with instant filtering.

Privacy & Full Control

You own your memory bank. Search, edit, export in JSON, or permanently delete individual memories or entire accounts at any time with one click.

Architecture Workflow

The 10-Step Memory Engine

From natural conversation to permanent, vectorized knowledge retrieval.

01

Chat & Extraction

User messages are analyzed by the LLM to identify durable personal facts across 10 categories.

02

Conflict Validation

Facts are structured as validated JSON, checked for duplicates, and cross-referenced with past history.

03

Vector Embedding

Approved facts are converted into high-dimensional vector embeddings and stored in PostgreSQL pgvector.

04

Grounded Retrieval

Future queries trigger hybrid semantic search to ground replies in your real personal context.
